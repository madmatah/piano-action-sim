# Vocabulary

The trade's words, in English and in French, as the interface, the documents and the code use them. Each rule ends with its check, or says it is *not mechanically checked*.

- **The owner is the authority on the trade's vocabulary.** A term is his word, or the word of the source he points to, as he gives it; a word taken from the question put to him is not his ruling. A term in doubt is asked, not settled by an agent. *Not mechanically checked.*
- **One mapping.** The French–English mapping and the code identifiers are `docs/research/A3-nomenclature-and-regulation.md` > Bilingual glossary FR ↔ EN (+ DE) and code identifiers, its open questions in `docs/vocabulary-worksheet.md`. Nothing else restates it: a document, a catalog or a comment uses its terms and points to it. *Not mechanically checked.*
- **No source is cited for a trade term**: the trade's vocabulary belongs to the trade. *Not mechanically checked.*
- **Trade quantities and the owner's names**: the interface and the documents speak in the quantities a technician reads (down weight, key dip, let-off) and name the parts as the glossary does, in English and in French alike; a code identifier is the glossary's. *Not mechanically checked.*
